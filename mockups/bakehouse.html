We mill on Mondays.

The grain comes from two farms within sixty miles, and it goes through our own stone mill at the back of the shop. Flour that fresh behaves differently, so the doughs are mixed slower and rested longer.

Everything is baked overnight. What is on the shelf at seven is what came out at four.
