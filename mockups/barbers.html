Before you come in

Do you take card?

Card, cash, or phone. All the same to us.

Can I bring my son?

Yes. Under twelves are 18, and they can sit in chair one where the clippers are quietest.

How far ahead do you book?

About two weeks. Saturdays go first, so book those early.

What if I am late?

Ring us. We will hold the chair ten minutes, then it goes to a walk in.
