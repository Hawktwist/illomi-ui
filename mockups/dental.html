Common questions

Are you taking new patients?

Yes, for both routine care and urgent appointments.

Do you take my insurance?

We invoice most major plans directly. Bring your details to the first visit.

What if I am nervous?

Say so when you book. We allow longer, explain each step, and stop whenever you ask.

Do you see children?

From age three, in the same chair as everyone else.

In pain today?

Two slots are held every weekday for urgent appointments. Ring before ten and we can nearly always see you the same day.
